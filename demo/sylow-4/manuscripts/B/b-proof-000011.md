- 标签：Sylow p-子群唯一当且仅当正规

## 命题

设 G 是有限群，p 是素数，P 是 G 的一个 Sylow p-子群。则 P 在 G 中唯一（即 G 恰有一个 Sylow p-子群）当且仅当 P 在 G 中正规；特别地，若 G 是交换群，则它的 Sylow p-子群唯一。

## 证明

If a Sylow p-subgroup is unique, then it is equal to all its conjugations and thus normal. If there are multiple Sylow p-subgroups, they must be conjugate to each other, so none of them can be closed under conjugation, prohibiting normality. Since any subgroup of an abelian group is normal, a Sylow p-subgroup must be unique.
