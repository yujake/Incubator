## 3. 断言清单（每条带引用）

### 3.1 3

**`claim-1`**｜证据档位 `cross_check`｜引用：`stmt:000060`

Let G be a finite group and p a prime with |G| = p^k m, where p does not divide m. Then G contains a Sylow p-subgroup of order p^k, and every p-subgroup of G is contained in some Sylow p-subgroup of G.

> ⚠️ 数值交叉验证，不构成证明

**`claim-2`**｜证据档位 `cross_check`｜引用：`stmt:000061`

Any two Sylow p-subgroups of a finite group G are conjugate: if H and K both have order p^k then K = gHg^{-1} for some g in G.

> ⚠️ 数值交叉验证，不构成证明

**`claim-3`**｜证据档位 `cross_check`｜引用：`stmt:000062`

Let |G| = p^k m with p not dividing m and let n_p be the number of Sylow p-subgroups of G. Then n_p divides m and n_p is congruent to 1 modulo p.

> ⚠️ 数值交叉验证，不构成证明

**`claim-4`**｜证据档位 `cross_check`｜引用：`stmt:000063`

For every Sylow p-subgroup P of a finite group G, the number of Sylow p-subgroups equals the index of the normalizer, n_p = [G : N_G(P)].

> ⚠️ 数值交叉验证，不构成证明

**`claim-5`**｜证据档位 `cross_check`｜引用：`stmt:000064`

A Sylow p-subgroup P of a finite group G is the unique Sylow p-subgroup if and only if P is normal in G; in particular, if G is abelian then each of its Sylow subgroups is unique.

> ⚠️ 数值交叉验证，不构成证明
