## 3. 断言清单（每条带引用）

### 3.1 1

**`claim-1`**｜证据档位 `heuristic`｜引用：`lit:000000`

Ball determined the largest hyperplane section of the unit cube: among all hyperplane sections of the cube, a section perpendicular to 2^{-1/2}(1,1,0,...,0) has the largest volume.

**`claim-2`**｜证据档位 `heuristic`｜引用：`lit:000001`

Koenig studies the largest hyperplane sections of the unit ball of l_p^n for p > 2 and shows that the cube-slicing phenomenon persists for suitable normals when p is large.

**`claim-3`**｜证据档位 `heuristic`｜引用：`lit:000002`、`lit:000003`、`lit:000006`

The literature on plane sections of the unit ball of l_p, on critical central sections of the cube and on extremal sections of subspaces of l_p supplies the general circle of questions addressed by the computations reported here.

### 3.2 2

**`claim-4`**｜证据档位 `proof_review`｜引用：`stmt:000034`

For every p in [1,infinity) and every unit vector n, the area A_p(n) of the central section B_p^3 intersected with the plane n^perp depends only on that plane, is invariant under signed coordinate permutations, and equals (1/2) integral_0^{2 pi} ||cos(psi) u + sin(psi) v||_p^{-2} dpsi for any orthonormal basis {u,v} of n^perp.

### 3.3 3 Main results

**`claim-5`**｜证据档位 `cross_check`｜引用：`stmt:000034`、`exp:000003`

For every p in [1,infinity) the coordinate normal gives A_coord(p) = 4 Gamma(1+1/p)^2 / Gamma(1+2/p); this equals pi at p = 2 and tends to 4 as p tends to infinity, and the quadrature kernel reproduces these values to relative deviation at most 1e-13.

> ⚠️ 数值交叉验证，不构成证明

**`claim-6`**｜证据档位 `cross_check`｜引用：`stmt:000034`、`stmt:000042`、`lit:000002`、`lit:000006`

For every p in [1,infinity), the central plane section of the unit ball of l_p^3 by the hyperplane with face-diagonal normal (1,1,0)/sqrt(2) has area A_face(p) = 2^{1/2-1/p} * 4 Gamma(1+1/p)^2 / Gamma(1+2/p), with A_face(1) = sqrt(2), A_face(2) = pi and A_face(p) tending to 4 sqrt(2); moreover A_face(p) < A_coord(p) exactly when p < 2, with equality at p = 2 and the reverse inequality for p > 2.

> ⚠️ 数值交叉验证，不构成证明

**`claim-7`**｜证据档位 `cross_check`｜引用：`stmt:000035`、`exp:000003`

If a unit normal n has at least one vanishing coordinate, then A_p(n) = C_p (sum over j not equal i of |n_j|^p)^{-1/p} with C_p = 4 Gamma(1+1/p)^2 / Gamma(1+2/p); consequently for 1 <= p < 2 every such normal gives A_p(n) <= C_p with equality only at coordinate normals, and for p > 2 the same expression holds with the reverse inequality.

> ⚠️ 数值交叉验证，不构成证明

**`claim-8`**｜证据档位 `proof_review`｜引用：`stmt:000041`、`exp:000003`

For every p in [1,infinity) the body-diagonal section area satisfies A_diag(p) = (3/4) integral_0^{2 pi} (|cos(theta - pi/6)|^p + |cos(theta + pi/6)|^p + |sin theta|^p)^{-2/p} d theta; moreover A_diag(1) = 3 sqrt(3)/4, A_diag(2) = pi, the limit of A_diag(p) as p tends to infinity is 3 sqrt(3), and p maps to A_diag(p) is continuous on [1,infinity).

**`claim-9`**｜证据档位 `proof_review`｜引用：`stmt:000042`、`stmt:000041`

The difference Delta(p) = A_face(p) - A_diag(p) is continuous on [1,infinity), Delta(2) = 0, Delta(1) = sqrt(2) - 3 sqrt(3)/4 > 0 and the limit of Delta(p) as p tends to infinity is 4 sqrt(2) - 3 sqrt(3) > 0; hence there is P0 in [1,infinity) with A_face(p) > A_diag(p) for every p >= P0.

**`claim-10`**｜证据档位 `cross_check`｜引用：`exp:000004`、`exp:000006`、`exp:000007`

Numerically supported strong conjecture, not established here: for the twelve values p in {1.05,1.1,1.2,1.5,2,2.5,3,4,5,8,16,1000} the largest central section of B_p^3 is attained at the coordinate normal when p < 2, at the body-diagonal normal when 2 < p < p-star and at the face-diagonal normal when p > p-star, where p-star = 4.998215596862.

> ⚠️ 数值交叉验证，不构成证明

**`claim-11`**｜证据档位 `cross_check`｜引用：`exp:000006`、`exp:000007`

Two transition parameters separate the three regimes in the scanned range: the ratio A_diag/A_coord crosses 1 exactly at p = 2, and A_diag and A_face cross at p-star = 4.998215596862 located by bisection with six rounds of local refinement; along the boundary arcs of normals of the form (0,1,t) and (1,1,t) the section area is largest at an endpoint t in {0,1,infinity}, and any strictly intermediate t gives a strictly smaller value for p not equal to 2.

> ⚠️ 数值交叉验证，不构成证明

### 3.4 4

**`claim-12`**｜证据档位 `cross_check`｜引用：`exp:000003`

The quadrature kernel used for all scans was validated independently: it returns pi for every central section at p = 2 with deviation at most 1e-15, matches the octahedron clipping values at p = 1, approaches the cube values 4, 3 sqrt(3) and 4 sqrt(2) at p = 1000, reproduces 4 Gamma(1+1/p)^2 / Gamma(1+2/p) to relative deviation at most 1e-13, and is invariant under rotation of the plane basis to 1e-12.

> ⚠️ 数值交叉验证，不构成证明

**`claim-13`**｜证据档位 `cross_check`｜引用：`lit:000000`、`lit:000001`、`stmt:000042`

The three-regime picture obtained here is a three-dimensional finite-p counterpart of the cube-slicing result of Ball and of the p > 2 analysis of Koenig: as p tends to infinity the three candidate areas tend to 4, 3 sqrt(3) and 4 sqrt(2), so the face-diagonal normal is the largest in the limit, in agreement with the cube case.

> ⚠️ 数值交叉验证，不构成证明

**`claim-14`**｜证据档位 `cross_check`｜引用：`exp:000004`、`exp:000007`

Limitation: no analytic argument is offered here for the absence of intermediate maximizing normals; the evidence is a boundary-arc scan together with a grid in the ordered absolute-value domain 0 <= a <= b <= c, so a normal with three distinct non-zero coordinates lying outside the scanned grid is not excluded by these computations.

> ⚠️ 数值交叉验证，不构成证明
