1. Introduction
The Busemann-Petty problem and the hyperplane conjecture of Bourgain initi-
ated an investigation of extremal hyperplane sections of convex bodies. This became
a very active area in convex geometry and geometric tomography.
K. Ball [B] found the maximal hyperplane section of the n-cube in a celebrated
paper.
The minimal ones had been determined by Hadwiger [Ha] and Hensley
[He]. Meyer and Pajor [MP] settled the maximal section problem for the ln
p-balls, if
0 < p < 2, and the minimal one for the ln
1 -ball, which was extended by Koldobsky
[K] to ln
p-balls for 0 < p < 2. Important progress in the case 2 < p < ∞was made
recently by Eskenazis, Nayar and Tkocz [ENT]. Webb [W] found the maximal cen-
tral sections of the regular n-simplex. The paper by Nayar and Tkocz [NT] gives
an excellent survey on extremal sections of classical convex bodies.
For non-central sections at distance t to the centroid, not too many results are
known. Moody, Stone, Zach and Zvavitch [MSZZ] proved that the maximal sections
of the n-cube at very large distances from the origin are those perpendicular to the
main diagonal. Pournin [P] showed the same result for slightly smaller distances.
Liu and Tkocz [LT] proved a corresponding result for the ln
1 -ball, if the distance t to
the origin satisﬁes
