## 1. 目录与文件组织体例

### 1.1 成文规范:每卷是一个"自包含单元"

这套书把体例写成了规范文件,而不是口头约定:`PM/每卷文件夹自包含规范.md`(2026-09-07 生效,
`PM/每卷文件夹自包含规范.md:3-6`)。其标准结构如下(逐字引用):

> ```
> 第 X 卷_卷名/
> ├── README.md                # 卷目录与状态(含卷末衔接固定节,见 §6)
> ├── 第 X 章_主题.md           # 正文(每章 ≥4000 汉字等规格另见写作规范)
> ├── 卷内引用/                 # 本卷正文引用的全部自产材料(见 §3)
> │   ├── <材料名>.md|.txt      # 证明转写、核对档、数值证据摘要等
> │   └── ...
> ├── archive_v1_20260907/     # v1 归档(重写前确认归档)
> └── (draft/ 等草稿目录完成清理后不留于正式卷)
> ```
>
> —— `PM/每卷文件夹自包含规范.md:17-26`

规范 §1 说明了这样做的理由(`PM/每卷文件夹自包含规范.md:8-13`):"每一卷文件夹本身是一个**自包含单元**:
读者打开该卷目录即可读完全卷,正文引用的所有自产材料……都**在该卷文件夹内**,不依赖工作根 `analysis/`
或其他卷目录。工作根 `analysis/` 只承载研究过程记录(cycle 报告、计划、草稿、跨卷核对),**不是正文的引用目标**。"

### 6.4 论文线(单篇)的检查/审校机制 —— 这部分与 G/H 最接近

`PM/analysis/style/` 是一整套"最严苛审稿人"流程,`PM/analysis/style/RUBRIC.md:3-7` 说明其形态:

> Purpose: a single, executable standard for the "most severe referee" pass over
> `research_papers/{…}`. **One referee agent per paper** writes `analysis/style/<paper>_REFEREE.md`.

**输出字段(逐字)**:

> Each finding gets: axis letter, severity, a **line number** (of the paper's `main.tex`),
> a **verbatim quote** of ≤20 words, the **minimal fix** (a concrete replacement/deletion), and
> `maths: no|yes` (yes only if the fix changes mathematics; such items go to the content track,
> not the style track).
>
> —— `STYLE/RUBRIC.md:30-33`

**五个攻击轴 + 可执行判据**(`RUBRIC.md:35-94`):A 多余定义/记号/一次性对象(A1–A5);B 未展开证明步(B1–B5);
C 迂回冗长(C1–C5);D 脚手架被删/直觉丢失(D1–D5);E 词汇与语域(E1–E5)。其中 B2 明确列出**英文禁词表**
(见 §7.3),C4 要求"**必须给出更短路线**,否则不许报 C4"(`:65-67`)。

**严重度与验收线**(逐字):

> | severity | meaning | action |
> |---|---|---|
> | **fatal** | a proof step that is false or unverifiable; a contradiction; a statement that cannot be parsed | must be fixed before any submission |
> | **severe** | B1/B2 where content is missing; a C4 detour; an A1/A2 definition with `uses = 1`; D1/D2 on a main theorem; E1 in a theorem statement | fix in this pass |
>
> **Bar for "clean"**: zero fatal, and every severe item either fixed or explicitly argued down here
> with a stated reason. A referee who finds nothing in an axis must say which checks produced the
> empty verdict (e.g. "A: 41 symbols counted, all used ≥2 times").
>
> —— `STYLE/RUBRIC.md:98-107`

**审稿报告模板**(逐字,`RUBRIC.md:111-126`):

```markdown
# <paper> — style referee
Hash of main.tex reviewed: <sha256>  |  pages: <n>  |  lines: <n>
## Verdict
<one paragraph: is the paper readable to Halmos/Serre standard; the two worst offenders>
## Findings
| # | axis | severity | line | quote (≤20 words) | minimal fix | maths |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
## Axis audits that came back clean
- A: <procedure + numbers>
...
## Shorter route proposed (C4 only)
<explicit replacement steps, or "none">
## Top five fixes in priority order
```

**审稿人禁止事项**(逐字,`RUBRIC.md:128-134`):

> * Do not rewrite the paper; propose the minimal edit.
> * Do not report a finding without a line number and a quote.
> * Do not request a change of mathematics (that is a different track); label it `maths: yes` and
>   move on.
> * Do not pad: a 5-line finding is a finding; a 40-line essay is not.

**"轴查空"也必须自证清白**(实测范例,`STYLE/p6_quotient_lattice_torus_REFEREE.md:46-56`):
"A4 is *not* empty (F14, F15); the empty sub-rule of A is none other than the "heavier than needed" half of A4,
which I checked and rejected: max subscript depth over the paper is 3 … **Procedure**: collected the preamble
primitives (22–29: …), then counted hits with `[regex]::Matches` on the literal: `\one` 25, `\T` 49, `\Z` 85, …
21 symbols total, all reaching ≥2 uses except `\e` (1, = F6) and the $T[q]$ half of Definition 5.2 (1, = F7)."

**修/审分离协议**(`STYLE/FIX_PROTOCOL.md:1-29`,逐字要点):

> 1. **Freeze during refereeing.** No edit to any `research_papers/*/main.tex` while a referee agent
>    is reading it. Each referee report pins the SHA-256 it reviewed; if the hash it reports differs
>    from the file's, the report is re-checked against the current revision before any fix.
> 2. **One edit pass per paper.** … Never edit a paper twice in the same round; the second pass would
>    invalidate the first pass's line references.
> 3. **Verify before adopting.** Every referee finding is re-checked here… **Two findings were false in
>    the previous round, and adopting them would have broken a correct paper.** Rejected findings are
>    recorded with the reason…
> 4. **Style fixes change no mathematics.** …
> 5. **Per-paper acceptance test after the pass** `pdflatex -interaction=batchmode main.tex` ×3;
>    scan `main.log` for `Overfull|Underfull|Undefined|LaTeX Error|^! |Citation`; record the new page
>    count and the new SHA-256…
> 7. **Record per paper**: a new section `## Style pass (…)` in `REVIEW.md` with (a) findings accepted
>    and applied…; (b) findings rejected with reasons; (c) page count and hash before/after.

**独立审计脚本基线**(`FIX_PROTOCOL.md:31-39`):`vocab_audit.py`(E 轴词表,逐篇逐词计数)、
`definition_audit.py`(A 轴:定义环境、宏声明、一次性宏 —— 实测报出"dead macros"),`proof_length_audit.py`
(C/D 轴:证明跨度、最长的陈述+证明对)。每个审计"在修完之后重跑并记录差值"(`:39`)。

**每篇论文的产物集**(实测 `RP/<paper_id>/`,11 篇一致):
`main.tex` + `main.pdf` + `main.aux/log/out` + `NOTES.md`(阶段 A 契约)+ `EVIDENCE.md` +

## 10. 我们能直接照搬的 10 条

1. **一条断言 = 一个稳定唯一编号 + 一个状态标签**(`证`/`验`/`待精化`/`作废`),编号只在单一权威台账里维护,重编号必须留映射(`U0:6`、`:16`;勘误案例 `V10/勘误记录.md:266-281`)。
2. **台账行必须能定位两件事**:证明在哪个文件哪一节(`U0:75` 的"见 H2.3"),数值证据是什么脚本、什么范围、几组、零反例几组(`U0:161`)。
3. **正文只写结论,路径与复跑命令落进"证据索引 + 台账",并用脚本核对"点名即存在"**(`V10/卷内引用/证据索引.md:3`;`check_yan_anchors.py:1-5`)。
4. **台账由脚本生成,不手工维护**;索引里每条给"断言所在文件 → 材料 → 复跑命令"(`r75_index.py:2-7`、`:59-63`)。
5. **禁词表机器扫描**:中文 15 词 + 英文 12 连词,命中打印上下文供人工裁决,并允许"元叙述"豁免(`series_banned_words.py:16-22`;`STYLE/RUBRIC.md:50-53`)。
6. **数值证据必须写明搜索域和"漏掉了什么类";存在性否定不得由有限网格支撑**(`V10/卷内引用/桥的数值证据.md:38-41`;`V10/勘误记录.md:693`)。
7. **勘误条目固定字段**:类别代号 + 处置状态 + 位置 + 原文 + 更正后 + 逐文件影响清单 + 教训;假警报与自我纠错同样入册(`V10/勘误记录.md:3-4`、`:266-279`、`:316-336`、`:696-706`)。
8. **收口章固定四件**:定理速查 → 开放问题(已知边界 + 攻击路线)→ 数值核对总账(必带"复核口径"段)→ 卷末/上游衔接(`V4/第6章…md:11/85/125/158`;`V10/第5章…md:12/96/153/217`)。
9. **审校发现必须是"轴 + 严重度 + 行号 + ≤20 词逐字引文 + 最小修法 + 是否改数学",且报告钉住被审版本 hash**;轴"零发现"也要自证过程与数字(`STYLE/RUBRIC.md:30-33`、`:113`、`:105-107`)。
10. **审/修分轮**:审校期间冻结待审文件,一次改完一篇(避免二次编辑使行号失效),每条发现采纳前先复核(上一轮有 2 条发现是假的)(`STYLE/FIX_PROTOCOL.md:5-16`)。
