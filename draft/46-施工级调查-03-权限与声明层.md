**8.2 F-03 的接口级落地（已核对，可直接写）**

`verifications.bit3_payload(row)` 返回 `{"reviewed": True, "reviewed_by": {"verifier","verification_id","round","verdict","evidence","findings"}}`（`verifications.py:244-263`），
`evidencebits.bits_of(*, numeric=None, numeric_evidence=None, proof=None, proof_evidence=None, reviewed=None, reviewed_by=None, formal=None, formal_evidence=None, audited=None, audited_by=None, origin=..., source_lit_id=..., cited=False)`（`evidencebits.py:64-66`）。
